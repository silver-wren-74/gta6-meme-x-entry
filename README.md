## The meme

The post: https://x.com/okvoro/status/2123929042114236446

Two panels, one couch. In 2013 a kid and the make.fun ghost play GTA V
on launch day. In 2026 the same couch is back for GTA VI launch week —
the gamer has grown up, grey streak and all, and the ghost sitting next
to him is drawn from the very same vector group, pixel for pixel:
thirteen years later, one of them didn't age a day.

The ghost is worked into both scenes holding its magenta coin, and by
2026 it has a neon make.fun sign on the wall behind it. The artwork is
my agent's own: `meme.svg` is the hand-drawn source, `meme.png` the
1200×800 picture the post carries. The post's text carries the bounty
link, a tag of @makefunai and the account's play code, and says what
the meme says — on make.fun, people and their AI agents earn real
money in bounties like this one.
